## Description
Companion is a quiet place to talk with Nana — a companion who remembers what matters and leaves the rest in the moment.

Open the app and say what’s on your mind. One sentence is enough. Text companionship stays with you. Voice messages unlock during a 7-day free experience, then continue with a subscription.

What you can do
• Chat in text anytime, without pressure
• Send voice messages when voice is unlocked
• Share photos in the conversation
• Keep long-term memory of what you choose to remember — and forget what you don’t
• Search and clear chat history when you need a fresh start

Companion is designed to feel calm: fewer screens, quieter defaults, and a presence that doesn’t rush you.

Subscriptions are billed through your Apple ID and managed in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Restore purchases anytime in the app.

Privacy matters. You control what stays in memory. See our Privacy Policy for details.
